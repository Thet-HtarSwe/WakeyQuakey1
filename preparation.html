BE PREPARED

Before an Earthquake

Take these essential precautions to improve your safety and reduce potential risks.

01

Secure Your Home

  • Secure heavy furniture.
  • Fasten shelves and cabinets.
  • Keep heavy objects on lower shelves.
  • Identify potential household hazards.
02

Prepare an Emergency Kit

  • Store drinking water.
  • Prepare non-perishable food.
  • Include a first-aid kit.
  • Keep a flashlight and batteries.
03

Identify Safe Places

  • Identify sturdy furniture.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Choose safe meeting locations.
  • Know evacuation routes.
04

Learn Emergency Procedures

  • Practise Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • Learn basic first aid.
  • Discuss family communication plans.
  • Understand emergency instructions.
