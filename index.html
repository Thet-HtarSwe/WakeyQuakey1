HOW EARTHQUAKES WORK

Understand the science. Prepare for the unexpected.

Earthquakes are natural events caused by the sudden release of energy within the Earth. Understanding how they happen is the first step toward reducing their risks.

Tectonic plates

Moving slabs of crust that grind, collide and slip past each other.

Seismic waves

Energy that travels through the ground and shakes the surface.

Magnitude

A scale that measures how much energy a quake releases.
