Knowledge Today. Safety Tomorrow.

Building earthquake awareness through knowledge, preparation, and action.

OUR MISSION

To provide clear, reliable, and accessible earthquake education that helps communities understand risk, prepare before disasters, and make safe decisions.

OUR VISION

A future where people are better informed, prepared for emergencies, and empowered with the knowledge to reduce disaster risks and protect lives.

CORE VALUES

01

Accuracy & Trust

We are committed to sharing accurate and reliable earthquake information from trustworthy sources.

02

Accessible Education

We aim to make earthquake science clear and accessible, so everyone can understand risks and safety measures.

03

Preparedness & Responsibility

We encourage daily preparedness and responsible actions to help individuals and communities stay safe during earthquakes.
